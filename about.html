Our Story

From a small workshop to a showroom people love

UrbanHaven began in 2011 in a modest workshop with two carpenters and one belief: furniture should be made to be lived with. Today our showroom brings together artisans, designers and families who want pieces that grow more beautiful with every year.

Every sofa, bed and table is shaped from responsibly sourced wood and natural fabrics, then finished by hand, so what arrives in your home carries real care.

  • Independent, family-run since day one
  • Handcrafted by skilled local artisans
  • Designed to last decades, not seasons

To make beautifully crafted, durable furniture accessible, and to help every family build a home that feels calm, personal and welcoming.

How We Work

From first sketch to your living room

A thoughtful four-step process that keeps quality in focus from start to finish.

Design

Ideas are sketched and refined with our designers around real homes and real needs.

Source

We select solid wood and natural fabrics from trusted, responsible suppliers.

Craft

Artisans shape, join and finish each piece by hand, checking quality at every stage.

Deliver

White-glove delivery and assembly, with packaging taken away when we leave.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way
