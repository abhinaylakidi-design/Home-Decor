Styling Tips

Style like a designer

Three simple ideas from our interior team to help your accessories look intentional.

01

Group in odd numbers

Cluster three objects of different heights for an arrangement that feels balanced and natural.

02

Layer textures

Mix linen, wood, ceramic and woven fibres so a calm palette still feels rich and tactile.

03

Play with light

Place mirrors opposite windows and add warm lamps to make every room glow in the evening.
