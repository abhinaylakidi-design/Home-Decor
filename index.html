About UrbanHaven

A showroom built around how you actually live

UrbanHaven started with a simple belief: a home should feel warm, personal and effortless. We work with skilled artisans and sustainable materials to create furniture that looks beautiful today and holds up for decades.

  • Responsibly sourced solid wood and natural fabrics
  • Pieces you can see, touch and test in person
  • Friendly designers on hand for every budget
